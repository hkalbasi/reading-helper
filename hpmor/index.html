Method

  1. Source: Chapter 7 from the public HPMOR mirror (hpmor.info/chapter/7, cross-checked with LessWrong / hpmor.com excerpts); Chapters 8–10 from the author's LessWrong posts (cross-checked with an hpmor.com/chapter/8 Wayback capture), because hpmor.com is Cloudflare-protected.
  2. Text split into sections of 1–5 paragraphs (smaller preferred; long paragraphs stand alone): Ch7 → 91 sections, Ch8 → 51 sections, Ch9 → 28 sections, Ch10 → 42 sections.
  3. Column 1 (verbatim English) is copied byte-for-byte and mechanically verified: python3 scripts/verify.py checks all paragraphs match data/ch7_source.json (332), data/ch8_source.json (187), data/ch9_source.json (84) and data/ch10_source.json (142).
  4. Column 2 is an English summary (1–3 sentences). Column 3 is a close-to-verbatim Persian translation. Column 4 is a Persian summary.

Verification

Run: python3 scripts/verify.py — it must print OK: 332/332 (ch7), OK: 187/187 (ch8), OK: 84/84 (ch9) and OK: 142/142 (ch10) paragraphs exact match, plus SHA-256 hashes of the concatenated verbatim text.
