Chapters

  • Chapter 7: Reciprocation — Harry & Draco meet on the Hogwarts Express platform; reciprocation, blood-purity debate, science vs magic, moon landing, and Draco's letter to Lucius. (91 sections, 332 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 8: Positive Bias — Harry meets Hermione on the train; the vanishing-drink experiment, the 2-4-6 task and positive bias, Neville's lost toad, the Lords of Chaos prank, and consequentialism. (51 sections, 187 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 9: Title Redacted, Part I — The Sorting ceremony; staff-table observations, Flitwick's goblin ancestry, Hermione to Ravenclaw, Neville to Hufflepuff, Draco to Slytherin, Harry's cheered walk and resolve, and the Hat's cliffhanger reply. (28 sections, 84 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 10: Self Awareness, Part II — Harry's telepathic debate with the Sorting Hat; self-awareness, secrecy, House deliberation, the SLYTHERIN shock, and "Just kidding! RAVENCLAW!". (42 sections, 142 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 11: Omake Files 1, 2, 3 — Three non-canonical extras; Harry reports his scar pain to Dumbledore and plots against "You-Know-Who", a Dementor parody, and the Hat's Headmaster's-office gag. (47 sections, 144 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 12: Impulse Control — After the Sorting, Ravenclaws pester Harry about the Hat; deflections, McGonagall's threat joke, and settling into Ravenclaw. (41 sections, 142 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 13: Asking the Wrong Questions — Harry wakes late to a memory-wiped puzzle game with point notes; navigating Hogwarts, rescuing Neville from Slytherin bullies with pies, Sprout's rescue, and an apology. (55 sections, 269 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 14: The Unknown and the Unknowable — Harry reports the Sorting Hat's secret Slytherin message to McGonagall; warnings, secrecy, and the road to the Time-Turner. (34 sections, 149 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 15: Conscientiousness — First Transfiguration lesson; Harry struggles with practical magic while Hermione shines, and McGonagall's money-pledge. (25 sections, 108 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 16: Lateral Thinking — Quirrell's first Defence lesson with Quirrell points; Harry, Draco and Hermione tested on violence, creativity, and lateral solutions. (49 sections, 223 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 17: Locating the Hypothesis — Time-Turner experiments and the prime-factor message; a wand standoff over Neville's Remembrall, and Dumbledore's ominous Potions-book secret. (93 sections, 454 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 18: Dominance Hierarchies — Potions with Snape and the Weasley twins' warnings; a classroom explosion, Slytherin politics, and a compromise brokered with Dumbledore. (79 sections, 373 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 19: Delayed Gratification — Quirrell's Defence lesson on losing and dominance contests; Harry humiliated by older Slytherins, learns to lose, earns fifty-one points, and leads cheers for Slytherin. (61 sections, 295 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 20: Bayes's Theorem — Harry recovers with Quirrell; Bayesian evidence, the dark side, ambition and science, a star-vision, Dumbledore's Legilimency, the Occlumency deal, and the Pioneer plaque secret. (47 sections, 228 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 21: Rationalization — Hermione wins the book-reading contest and claims a date as forfeit; Harry founds the Bayesian Conspiracy with Draco, receives an unsigned warning note, and witnesses Trelawney's interrupted prophecy. (56 sections, 236 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 22: The Scientific Method — Harry's first experiment falsifies his theory that spell forms are arbitrary, then he and Draco realize magic itself may be fading and design tests to tell fading magic from weakening blood and lost knowledge. (69 sections, 302 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 23: Belief in Belief — Harry teaches Draco genetics to test whether magic is fading, but the experiments destroy Draco's belief in blood purism instead — and Draco answers science with a torture spell. (55 sections, 218 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 24: Machiavellian Intelligence Hypothesis — Draco confronts Harry over his cursed hand and, instead of revenge, plots to master Harry's rationality and seize the Conspiracy for himself while Harry muses on the Machiavellian origins of human intelligence. (45 sections, 182 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 25: Hold Off on Proposing Solutions — Harry traces magic to an engineered Source keyed to a DNA marker, then tasks Fred and George with planting a decoy story for Rita Skeeter while Quirrell coldly intercepts her. (55 sections, 224 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 26: Noticing Confusion — Quirrell's fury over an ignorantly cast curse, the fake Harry–Ginny betrothal that humiliates Rita Skeeter, a Gringotts outing, lunch in Mary's Room, and the stolen diary of Roger Bacon. (53 sections, 259 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 27: Empathy — Fallout from the prank with the Obliviated twins, Occlumency under a perfect Occlumens, Snape and Hermione, bullies tormenting Lesath Lestrange, and Quirrell on Azkaban under the stars. (92 sections, 389 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 28: Reductionism — Harry and Hermione test the limits of Transfiguration, reductionism and quantum foundations, Hermione's reckless hallway run, and a Potions aftermath with Snape. (89 sections, 291 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 29: Egocentric Bias — Hermione's fame frustrations and date with Harry, an hour in Quirrell's web, war planning for the coming battles, and a lonely Halloween anniversary. (64 sections, 250 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 30: Working in Groups, Pt 1 — The first forest battle of Quirrell's war game: Dragon Army, the Chaos Legion, and the Sunshine Regiment fight for dominance. (72 sections, 267 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 31: Working in Groups, Pt 2 — Aftermath of the first battle: Harry and Draco puzzle over Hermione's victory until Draco realizes Quirrell assigned Blaise Zabini to advise her — the lesson is using other people's ideas. (13 sections, 47 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 32: Interlude: Personal Financial Management — Harry asks Dumbledore to release five Galleons for Christmas presents and is refused; money, Gringotts vaults, and financial independence. (11 sections, 40 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 33: Coordination Problems, Pt 1 — The second forest battle, fought in the lake: spies, feints, and Zabini's maneuvers end in a 254-to-254-to-254 three-way tie. (93 sections, 359 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 34: Coordination Problems, Pt 2 — Aftermath on the grand stage: Quirrell brokers the generals' pact, grants Christmas wishes, upbraids the parents, and Harry's attack on the Snitch starts a riot. (32 sections, 123 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 35: Coordination Problems, Pt 3 — Quirrell confronts Harry in his sealed office over his meddling, warns and dismisses Blaise, McGonagall reaches Hermione through a shining cat, and Draco extracts Harry's wish. (35 sections, 137 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 36: Status Differentials — Harry spends Christmas Eve caught between two families when his defense of Hermione's brilliance explodes at the Grangers' dinner table. (34 sections, 135 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 37: Interlude: Crossing the Boundary — On Christmas night Quirrell visits Harry, rebukes his meddling, and gifts him starlight as they cross into Christmas Day. (9 sections, 37 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 38: The Cardinal Sin — Harry returns to Hogwarts through a tense Platform confrontation where Lucius Malfoy probes his game with Draco before warning him privately. (29 sections, 117 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 39: Pretending to be Wise, Pt 1 — Harry's secret meeting with Dumbledore, where a Dementor-security puzzle gives way to a fierce debate over death, souls, and immortality. (53 sections, 206 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 40: Pretending to be Wise, Pt 2 — Over tea Quirrell warns Harry about Lucius-vs-Dumbledore politics and debates the afterlife. (11 sections, 43 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 41: Frontal Override — Harry lures Draco and Hermione into a rooftop chase with climbing gloves; Hermione slips, Draco grabs then drops her to win, and fallout follows. (28 sections, 112 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 42: Courage — Harry and Draco stage a fake fall from the roof as an apology to Hermione; Remus Lupin catches Harry and shares stories of James, Lily, Black and Pettigrew. (29 sections, 115 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 43: Humanism, Pt 1 — First-years practice the Patronus with Lupin but Harry fails; a Dementor comes to Hogwarts and forces Harry to relive his parents' murder. (66 sections, 263 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 44: Humanism, Pt 2 — Hollowed by Dementor exposure, Harry rejects comfort until Hermione's kiss snaps him out of the dark trance as Fawkes sings. (15 sections, 61 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 45: Humanism, Pt 3 — Harry sees the Dementor as Death itself and destroys it with a blinding human-shaped Patronus of defiance, stunning the Aurors. (37 sections, 147 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 46: Humanism, Pt 4 — Aftermath of the destroyed Dementor; sodas with Dumbledore and Quirrell, plotting talk on the walk back, Slytherin gossip about the kiss, and Harry's diamondlike deduction about the prophecy. (34 sections, 136 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 47: Personhood Theory — Draco confronts Harry over the Kiss and suspected plotting; Veritaserum, a pledge against Narcissa's killer, and a stunned realization about snakes. (79 sections, 316 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 48: Utilitarian Priorities — Harry agonizes over the sentience of snakes and plants over a vegetable breakfast, debates his inner House voices, and meets Hermione in the library. (20 sections, 77 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 49: Prior Information — Harry learns he is a Parselmouth; Quirrell theorizes Slytherin's Monster guards unwritable knowledge and reveals himself as a snake Animagus with a Time-Turner plot. (33 sections, 129 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 50: Self Centeredness — Invisible Harry scares Padma off rumor-mongering, Hermione freezes him out over his methods, and the days crawl toward Saturday's lunch with Quirrell. (29 sections, 114 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 51: Title Redacted, Pt 1 — Harry and Quirrell vanish from Mary's Place by Cloak and Time-Turner, then meet unobserved in a dark shop where Quirrell asks for help in a treasonous, illegal rescue of an innocent person in Azkaban — and Harry guesses Bellatrix. (27 sections, 106 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 52: The Stanford Prison Experiment, Pt 2 — Under Polyjuice and the Cloak, Harry flies through a storm to Azkaban, descends past the Dementors on his new Patronus, and finds the withered Bellatrix Black in the lowest cells. (25 sections, 100 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 53: The Stanford Prison Experiment, Pt 3 — Playing the Dark Lord with the Death Eater password, Harry orders Bellatrix freed; a Polyjuiced servant swaps a corpse for her, setting the perfect crime of Bellatrix's faked death in motion. (12 sections, 48 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 54: The Stanford Prison Experiment, Pt 4 — As Bellatrix weeps and begs to die, Harry's Patronus flares out of control until a sense of doom recalls him; Auror Bahry intercepts them and Quirrell dismantles his every defense, until Harry's Patronus blocks a Killing Curse. (46 sections, 184 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 55: The Stanford Prison Experiment, Pt 5 — Demented and without his Patronus, Harry claws back his reason, revives Bellatrix, and walks past a prisoner's worst memory while refusing to destroy every Dementor and leaving a piece of his life behind; Emmeline Vance sends Dumbledore word. (62 sections, 246 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 56: TSPE, Constrained Optimization, Pt 6 — McGonagall's Patronus reaches Harry inside Azkaban; Dumbledore and Amelia hunt the escapees together as Harry hides Bellatrix under the Cloak, confronts his dark side's terror of death, and a dozen Dementors are sent after him. (31 sections, 123 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 57: TSPE, Constrained Cognition, Pt 7 — Harry wills the Dementors to stop and puts Bellatrix to sleep so only his expectations control them; Dumbledore finds Bahry alive, and Harry begins cutting open Azkaban's wall for his Muggle-rocket escape. (37 sections, 146 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 58: TSPE, Constrained Cognition, Pt 8 — Weighing the odds and arguing with Quirrell's snake over who should surrender, Harry awakens Quirrell, who hardens the rocket-broom with charms as Dumbledore gives chase and the trio blast out over the Dementor pit. (41 sections, 164 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 59: TSPE, Curiosity, Pt 9 — Rocket acceleration nearly kills them as Harry learns that Newton, not Aristotle, rules in flight; Bellatrix glimpses the Sun and clouds before the Pepper-Up sleep, and a wary healer takes her away under an Unbreakable Vow while Dumbledore goes back in time. (42 sections, 166 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 60: The Stanford Prison Experiment, Pt 10 — Back in the warehouse, Quirrell names Azkaban's indifference and warns Harry he is already the next Dark Lord, while Harry refuses to apologize; Dumbledore finds the "NO" note and Quirrell admits he does not know why Harry is different. (22 sections, 86 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 61: TSPE, Secrecy and Openness, Pt 11 — Minerva and Severus rush to Mary's Place and find Harry safe; the Order deduces Bellatrix's escape was Voldemort's work, Dumbledore declares the Second Wizarding War begun and confines Harry to Hogwarts, and McGonagall summons him for the Time-Turner test. (50 sections, 199 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 62: The Stanford Prison Experiment, Final — Dumbledore tells Harry that Bellatrix's escape marks the start of the war and that Harry is Voldemort's second requisite, confining him to Hogwarts; Fawkes shrieks at Harry to free Azkaban's prisoners, and Harry leaves weeping with the phoenix on his shoulder. (39 sections, 154 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 63: TSPE, Aftermaths — Fawkes chooses Hermione; Draco receives "I know it was you"; Neville vows to kill Bellatrix; Lesath Lestrange kneels to Harry; Moody and Snape poison Voldemort's grave; Harry realizes he has lost Quirrell as mentor and resolves to destroy Azkaban, while Trelawney wakes from a vast vision. (132 sections, 526 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 64: Omake Files 4, Alternate Parallels — The Council of Elrond out-thinks Sauron, the Pevensies debate Aslan, the Elements of Inquiry face Nightmare Moon, and other crossover parodies from Naruto, Twilight, Hamlet, Alice, and The Matrix. (37 sections, 146 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 65: Contagious Lies — Hermione notices Harry growing harder and more distant; McGonagall gets Hagrid pardoned and agrees to a thirty-hour day, while Quirrell's snake proposes staging a fake Dark Lord's return for Harry to defeat — and Harry weighs the cost of another deception. (32 sections, 126 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 66: Self Actualization, Pt 1 — Harry weighs Quirrell's plan to fake the Dark Lord's return, then the generals learn that Cedric Diggory is training Neville and Harry in dueling for the coming battle. (9 sections, 41 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 67: Self Actualization, Pt 2 — The Grey Knights of Chaos, wearing hidden weights and grey cloaks, crush the Sunshine Regiment; Daphne duels Neville with an Ancient Blade while Draco's Dragons are overwhelmed. (21 sections, 109 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 68: Self Actualization, Pt 3 — Quirrell rebalances the armies after Chaos's victory, and a wounded Hermione confronts Dumbledore about whether a girl can be a hero — and what it costs to live in Harry's shadow. (21 sections, 118 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 69: Self Actualization, Pt 4 — Hermione stuns three Slytherin bullies, rescues a Hufflepuff, and decides to become a hero; Harry worries she is copying him, while Daphne becomes the Sparkly Unicorn Princess of Sparklypoo. (26 sections, 122 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 70: Self Actualization, Pt 5 — Hermione founds S.P.H.E.W. and protests outside Dumbledore's office; Quirrell interrogates her ambition, Dumbledore claims heroes cannot be taught, and the new heroines decide to raid the forbidden corridor. (27 sections, 153 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 71: Self Actualization, Pt 6 — Hannah admits her crush on Neville, the S.P.H.E.W. girls hunt bullies, Daphne deduces that Millicent is a seer, and Snape hires a seventh-year to ambush Hermione. (29 sections, 142 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 72: SA, Plausible Deniability, Pt 7 — Harry lends Hermione the Cloak of Invisibility and an anonymous tip leads S.P.H.E.W. into a trap; Draco publicly cleans up Hermione and apologizes, then tells his father he is preparing for the next war. (56 sections, 279 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 73: SA, The Sacred and the Mundane, Pt 8 — Bully-hunting becomes routine until three older students ambush the girls with Hannah as hostage; Susan reveals inexplicable power and is hailed as a double witch, and Tonks is unmasked. (33 sections, 163 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 74: SA, Escalation of Conflicts, Pt 9 — Harry's threat and Tracey's fake dark ritual provoke a mass ambush and a forty-four-student battle; Dumbledore rebukes Harry for escalating the conflict, while Tracey claims to have eaten Pansy's soul. (54 sections, 269 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 75: Self Actualization Final, Responsibility — Slytherins plot revenge, Hermione confronts Harry over acting without asking, Snape dissolves S.P.H.E.W. and sentences her to detention, and Quirrell awards her a hundred points for doing what is right. (32 sections, 157 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 76: Interlude with the Confessor: Sunk Costs — Rianne meets Snape before her memories are erased; they discuss his regrets, the prophecy, and the choices that shape a life. (11 sections, 87 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 77: SA, Aftermaths: Surface Appearances — Dumbledore and Harry clash over justice, Quirrell probes Snape, Draco protects Millicent, and Hermione meets a mysterious figure. (21 sections, 236 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 78: Taboo Tradeoffs Prelude: Cheating — Parents and political leaders watch Hogwarts’ war games; Hermione’s actions draw Ministry scrutiny and an arrest. (34 sections, 407 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 79: Taboo Tradeoffs, Pt 1 — Hermione is arrested after Draco accuses her of attempted murder, while Harry and the adults struggle to understand what happened. (11 sections, 210 paragraphs, 4-column table)
  • Chapter 80: Taboo Tradeoffs, Pt 2, The Horns Effect — Hermione’s trial begins in the Wizengamot, where Harry faces political maneuvering and searches for a way to save her. (11 sections, 135 paragraphs, 4-column table)
