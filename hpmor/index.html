Method

  2. Text split into sections of 1–5 paragraphs (smaller preferred; long paragraphs stand alone): Ch7 → 91 sections, Ch8 → 51 sections.
  3. Column 1 (verbatim English) is copied byte-for-byte and mechanically verified: python3 scripts/verify.py checks all paragraphs match data/ch7_source.json (332) and data/ch8_source.json (187).
  4. Column 2 is an English summary (1–3 sentences). Column 3 is a close-to-verbatim Persian translation. Column 4 is a Persian summary.

Verification

Run: python3 scripts/verify.py — it must print OK: 332/332 (ch7) and OK: 187/187 (ch8) paragraphs exact match, plus SHA-256 hashes of the concatenated verbatim text.
